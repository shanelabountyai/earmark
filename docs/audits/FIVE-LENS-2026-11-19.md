### What a visitor holding the shared password can reach

`/demo` lists all 17 seeded demo users, including the owner, admin, finance and CMO of both orgs and Dana, and signs in as any of them with an MFA-satisfied session. They can perform every Earmark action the chosen role allows: requests, approvals, reallocations, invoices, matching, approval for payment, variance drafts and notes, fiscal years, programs, campaigns, lines and planned amounts, seats (create, assign, end, delegate), agencies and guest binding; plus the template's projects and share links, and reading the audit log and its CSV. They **cannot** create API keys or webhooks, set up SSO, invite, change roles or remove members, create or delete an org, export, change the AI cap, or offboard an agency. Shared state they can change for the next visitor: rules, seats, delegations, plan, agencies, Dana's agency, free text, and this month's AI allowance. Outside the app: email is off, webhooks go only to the app's own receiver, links typed into an invoice's file reference are shown to staff, and AI drafts reach a paid model only if a key is set in production (not verified). The gate covers every route except `/api/cron`, `/api/v1/*` and `/api/demo/webhook`, each of which has its own credential; it has no rate limit (S4).
